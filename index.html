A little about me

Over the years, I’ve had the opportunity to learn, grow, and work alongside some incredibly talented artists. My journey through animation and previsualization has taught me a lot, but one of the things I value most is knowing there’s always more to learn. That’s part of what keeps me excited about this craft.

Between studio work, I’ve continued to explore new ideas and develop independent projects, particularly in Unreal Engine. These projects give me room to experiment, challenge myself, and build on what I’ve learned. For me, creating isn’t something I only do when there’s a production to work on. It’s how I keep growing as an artist and stay connected to the things that inspire me.

I genuinely enjoy collaborating with other artists, sharing ideas, learning from one another, and working together to bring something to life that none of us could have made alone. I’ve been fortunate to contribute to projects at Nickelodeon Animation Studios and Noggin, and I’m grateful for the people I’ve met and the things they’ve taught me along the way.

I’m still learning, still experimenting, and still finding new reasons to love what I do. I’m grateful for every opportunity to create, and I’m excited to see where the next chapter takes me.
